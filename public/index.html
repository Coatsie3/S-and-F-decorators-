How we work

The finish starts long before the paint

Four stages, the same on every job, whether it's a single bedroom or a manor house.

Protect

  1. Protect your home

    Furniture covered, floors sheeted and fittings masked before a brush comes out. We keep the house clean and tidy every day.

    Dust sheets and masking on every job

  2. Prepare properly

    Filling, sanding, priming and repairs. Timber is taken back, cracks are cut out and new plaster gets a mist coat.

    Most of the finish happens here

  3. Paint with care

    Trade-quality paints chosen for the surface and Jersey's coastal weather, applied in full coats with sharp, clean lines.

    Two full coats as standard

  4. Finish and hand over

    We walk the job with you, touch up anything you spot and leave it ready to live in.

    Satisfaction guaranteed

About us

Built on 40 yearsof word of mouth

What started as one van and a passion for doing the job properly has grown into one of the island's most trusted decorating firms.

Much of our work still comes from repeat customers and their recommendations. We take pride in every project, from heritage restorations and period homes to modern kitchens and commercial premises.

We know Jersey's buildings and its weather. Salt air, driving rain and granite walls all call for the right products and the right preparation, and we choose both with that in mind.

FAQ

Common questions

Anything else, just give us a ring.

How much will the job cost?

Every project is different, so we quote on the size, condition and complexity of the work. After a free visit you'll get a clear written price with no hidden costs.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, free and with no obligation. Call 07797 799674 or send the form above and we'll arrange a time to come and look.

How long will the work take?

A single room usually takes one to two days. Larger projects can take a week or more. Your quote includes a timeline and we work to it.

Do you do interior and exterior work?

Yes, both. Living rooms, kitchens and hallways through to render, windows, doors, gates and timber.

Will you protect my furniture and floors?

Always. We cover furniture, sheet the floors and mask off anything not being painted, and leave the property clean and tidy when we finish.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance on every job.

Planning some decorating?

Book a free visit and we'll talk through colours, finishes and timings, with a written quote to follow.
